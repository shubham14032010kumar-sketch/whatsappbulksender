Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you get the most out of ShaktiX Enterprise.

How does the software send messages?

ShaktiX connects to your official WhatsApp Web session running in a secure, local Google Chrome automation driver. It automates chat loading, personalized text typing, and attachment sending just like a human assistant.

How does ShaktiX protect my account from restrictions?

While no tool can claim a 100% ban guarantee because WhatsApp enforces strict policies against unsolicited spam, ShaktiX provides industry-leading safety controls: randomized 8–18s human delays, multi-variant Spintax, daily pacing limits, and automatic opt-out handling.

Where do I find my Machine ID for activation?

Open ShaktiX on your Windows desktop, click the License / About tab in the menu, and copy your 8-character Machine ID (e.g. B55291AF). Send it to us to instantly issue your key.

Can I send images, PDFs, and video brochures?

Yes! You can attach marketing images, PDF catalogs, admission brochures, and product flyers alongside your personalized text caption.

Do I need technical or coding knowledge?

Not at all. The desktop application features a simple graphical interface (GUI). If you can use Excel and Chrome, you can launch campaigns in minutes.

How do I receive customer replies?

Replies appear directly on your phone's WhatsApp as normal. Furthermore, ShaktiX's 2-Way Auto-Responder listens on your desktop and can automatically reply with pricing or catalogs 24/7.
